02 / INTERPRET

How are candidate areas selected?

Each mapped feature represents roof geometry from Montréal's 2016 building dataset that overlaps economic-activity zones. The analysis screens for features near mapped local greenspace to help identify locations that may be suitable for future green rehabilitation or urban greening. Map colors show distance bands of 0–50 metres, 50–100 metres, and over 100 metres; the slider filters the results up to a maximum of 250 metres. These screening results do not confirm site suitability, property ownership, vacancy, or the feasibility of rehabilitation. The source is from 2016, so later construction or changes may be missing.

03 / SCOPE

What this map can—and cannot—tell us

This map identifies economic-activity areas near mapped greenspaces to support screening for possible green rehabilitation. It is a planning aid rather than a site-level feasibility study. The mapped features are based on roof geometry and a screening threshold, not parcel ownership, land-use intensity, public access, or the practical suitability of a rehabilitation project. Verify current conditions and site details with authoritative local data before advancing any intervention.
